Litterbug

Pickup data for cleaner places.

Litterbug is a beta mobile app for logging litter pickups, reviewing detector results, posting cleanup spots, sharing cleanup activity, and testing optional tips and bounties.

Privacy Policy Support

Scan

Use the camera to log litter items and review model suggestions.

Map

Create public cleanup spots and validate completed cleanups.

Share

Post cleanup summaries, photos, comments, and reactions in the community feed.

Payments

Test optional Stripe-backed tips, bounties, and payout flows during beta.
