Litterbug

A decentralized litter cleanup service for communities, by communities.

Litterbug is a gamified micro-gig and gig-ecology application. It uses machine vision, gamified model training, and social media to quantify patterns of litter in the environment and their causes. Litterbugs and Litterbug supporters can coordinate through micro financial incentives that support the environment by reducing litter and the proximal causes of pollution.

Explore the field kit

Each screen supports a different part of a community cleanup—from finding litter to showing the collective impact.

01 / Scan

Spot it. Log it.

Point the camera at litter to identify and count what you collect, with a live record of the cleanup taking shape.

02 / Train

Teach the model.

Review the app’s suggestions and help improve recognition for the messy, real-world objects found outdoors.

03 / Progress

Build your impact.

Earn experience as your contributions make the data—and the local cleanup effort—stronger.

04 / Community

Share the work.

Turn a cleanup into a community post with photos, context, comments, and encouragement from fellow Litterbugs.

05 / Map

Find a place to help.

See public cleanup spots, coordinate local action, and follow the progress of shared environmental work.

06 / Profile

Keep your record.

Track the items you have collected, your activity, and the contribution you bring to the collective.

07 / Leaderboard

Celebrate the collective.

See how individual effort adds up across the community and recognize the people moving the work forward.

Swipe sideways to explore all seven screens.
