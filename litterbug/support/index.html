Common Issues

Camera or scan is not working

  • Confirm camera permission is enabled in iOS Settings.
  • Use a physical device. Litterbug's camera and model features are not designed for Expo Go or simulator-only testing.
  • Restart the app if the camera view becomes stale.

Location or map is not working

  • Confirm Location permission is set to "While Using the App."
  • Litterbug does not require Always location access.
  • Be careful when posting cleanup spots because map pins may be public.

Payments, tips, bounties, or payouts

  • Beta payments run through Stripe sandbox unless you have been told otherwise.
  • Do not send real money during sandbox testing.
  • If a payout, bounty, or tip looks stuck, contact support with your username, the approximate time, and the action you tried.

Account Or Data Requests

  • Contact support from the email address used for your Litterbug account.
  • Include your username and the request type: account deletion, data access, payment issue, bug report, or feedback.
